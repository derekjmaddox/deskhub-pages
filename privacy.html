DeskHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Google Calendar Data

DeskHub requests read-only access to Google Calendar to retrieve and display upcoming calendar events on the user's private, self-hosted dashboard.

Data Storage

Google OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally on the user's Raspberry Pi. Calendar information is retrieved for display within DeskHub.

Data Sharing

DeskHub does not sell or share Google Calendar information with third parties. Calendar data is not published on this website.

Data Access and Revocation

Users can revoke DeskHub's Google Calendar access at any time through their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the DeskHub application developer through the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
